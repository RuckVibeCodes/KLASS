# KLASS Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## Target Founder Persona

**Primary Archetype: The Emerging Course Creator**

- **Subscriber Range:** 10K - 500K followers (sweet spot: 25K - 150K)
- **Platform:** YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or podcast-first creators
- **Niche:** Education, fitness, personal development, business, creative skills, tech tutorials
- **Revenue Stage:** Making $5K-50K/month from content but wants predictable recurring revenue

### Demographics
- Age: 25-45
- Gender: Any
- Location: English-speaking markets (US, UK, Canada, Australia)
- Tech-savvy enough to manage a platform, but not developers

---

## Pain Points (Why They Need KLASS)

### 1. **Platform Fees Eating Their Margins**
- Skool charges $99/month but takes no cut
- Kajabi, Teachable, Thinkific charge 5-10% transaction fees
- Patreon takes 8-12% of revenue
- "Why am I paying platform fees when I built the audience?"

### 2. **No True Ownership**
- Algorithm changes tank their reach overnight
- Platform ToS can shut them down without warning
- They don't own their member list or content
- "I'm building on rented land"

### 3. **Limited Customization**
- Can't fully brand the experience
- Stuck with platform's UI/UX decisions
- Features they need aren't prioritized
- "I want it to feel like MY community, not [Platform]'s"

### 4. **Fragmented Tech Stack**
- Course platform + community + email + payment = 4 tools
- Members confused by multiple logins
- Data siloed across systems
- "I spend more time on integrations than content"

### 5. **Scaling Anxiety**
- Current platform gets expensive at scale
- Support quality drops as they grow
- Features locked behind enterprise tiers
- "Will this work when I have 10,000 members?"

## Signs They're Ready to Switch

### Explicit Signals (What They Say)
- "Building my own community platform"
- "Launching a membership/course soon"
- "Frustrated with [Skool/Kajabi/Teachable/Patreon]"
- "Looking for alternatives to..."
- "Want more control over my business"
- "Tired of platform fees"

### Behavioral Signals (What They Do)
- Recently launched or talking about launching a course
- Promoting a Discord/Slack community (outgrowing free tools)
- Mentioning "community" in videos/posts frequently
- Asking audience for course/membership ideas
- Posting about hitting subscriber milestones
- Celebrating revenue milestones ($10K months, etc.)

### Timing Signals (When to Reach Out)
- Just hit 10K, 25K, 50K, or 100K subscribers
- Launching a new program/course
- End of contract with current platform
- After complaining about current tool publicly
- During "planning for next year" season (Q4)

## Content Niches to Prioritize

### Tier 1 (Highest Intent)
1. **Online Business/Entrepreneurship** - Already selling, understand SaaS
2. **Personal Finance/Investing** - Audience has money, used to subscriptions
3. **Career/Professional Development** - B2B-adjacent, higher LTV
4. **Marketing/Social Media** - Tech-savvy, build in public

### Tier 2 (High Potential)
5. **Fitness/Health Coaching** - Strong community culture
6. **Creative Skills** (design, video, music) - Portfolio-driven, loyal fans
7. **Tech Tutorials/Coding** - Will appreciate the platform
8. **Personal Development** - Transformation = recurring membership

### Tier 3 (Volume Play)
9. **Lifestyle/Productivity** - Broad audience, needs segmenting
10. **Parenting/Family** - Passionate communities
11. **Hobbies** (gaming, crafts, sports) - Engaged but lower LTV

---

## Disqualifying Factors (Who to Avoid)

❌ **Under 10K followers** - Not ready for paid community yet  
❌ **Pure entertainment** - No educational angle = hard to monetize  
❌ **Already locked into enterprise contracts** - Long sales cycle  
❌ **No existing audience** - They need to build first  
❌ **Tech companies/developers** - Will want to build their own  
❌ **Just want "exposure"** - Not serious about business

## Value Proposition for This ICP

### Lead With:
> "Own your community. Own your revenue. Build once, scale forever."

### Key Messages:
1. **Your Platform, Your Brand** - Fully white-labeled, your domain, your colors
2. **Zero Transaction Fees** - Keep 100% of what you earn (Stripe fees only)
3. **All-in-One** - Community + courses + resources + events in one place
4. **Built for Creators** - Not enterprise software adapted for creators

### Compare Against:
| Pain Point | Skool | KLASS |
|------------|-------|-------|
| Branding | Limited | Full white-label |
| Custom Domain | No | Yes |
| Transaction Fees | None | None |
| Monthly Cost | $99 | [Your pricing] |
| Ownership | Their platform | Your platform |

---

## Discovery Questions

When qualifying leads, ask:
1. "What's your current setup for courses/community?"
2. "What's your biggest frustration with it?"
3. "How important is branding to you?"
4. "What's your monthly revenue from community/courses?"
5. "If you could change one thing about your current platform, what would it be?"

## Outreach Cadence

1. **Day 0:** Initial personalized DM/email
2. **Day 3:** Follow-up if no response (value-add, not pushy)
3. **Day 7:** Final touch (case study or testimonial)
4. **Archive** if no response after Day 7

Focus on quality over quantity. 10 personalized outreaches > 100 spray-and-pray.
