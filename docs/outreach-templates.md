# KLASS Outreach Templates

> **Golden Rule:** Lead with value, not a pitch. Make them curious, not defensive.

---

## Cold DM Templates

### Instagram/Twitter DM - The Observer

```
Hey [Name]! Been following your content on [specific topic] - really resonated with your take on [specific point from recent content].

Quick question: I noticed you mentioned wanting to build a community for your audience. Are you still exploring options for that, or have you already figured it out?

Either way, love what you're building. 🙌
```

**Why it works:**
- Shows you actually consume their content
- References something specific (not generic)
- Opens with curiosity, not a pitch
- Low-pressure question

---

### Instagram/Twitter DM - The Peer

```
[Name] - just watched your video on [topic]. Legit the best breakdown I've seen.

Random q: are you using Skool/Kajabi for your community stuff? A few creator friends have been frustrated with the branding limitations and I'm curious what's working for people at your level.

No agenda, just genuinely curious!
```

**Why it works:**
- Positions you as a peer, not a salesperson
- References the pain point indirectly
- Creates opportunity for them to vent
- "No agenda" lowers guard

---

### Twitter DM - The Problem-Aware

```
Saw your tweet about [platform frustration]. Felt that.

We've been building something for creators who want actual ownership of their community platform - white-label, your domain, no transaction fees.

Not sure if it's a fit, but happy to show you what we're working on if you're curious. No pressure either way.
```

**Why it works:**
- Responds to explicit pain point
- Direct but not pushy
- "Not sure if it's a fit" = no pressure
- Focused on showing, not selling

---

### Instagram DM - The Milestone

```
Congrats on [50K/100K]! 🎉 That's a huge milestone.

Curious - are you thinking about launching a paid community or course for your audience? I work on a platform specifically for creators who want their own branded community space.

Might be way too early, but if you're ever exploring, happy to chat. Keep crushing it!
```

**Why it works:**
- Celebrates their win (genuine)
- Ties milestone to next logical step
- "Might be too early" = low pressure
- Ends on encouragement, not ask

## Email Templates

### Email - The Value-First Intro

**Subject:** Quick question about your community plans

```
Hi [Name],

I've been following your [YouTube channel/podcast/content] for a while - your [specific content piece] really stuck with me. You clearly know how to build an engaged audience.

I noticed you mentioned [building a community / launching a course / being frustrated with X platform] recently, and it got me thinking...

I'm working on KLASS - a white-label community platform built specifically for creators like you. Think Skool, but fully branded as yours, on your own domain, with zero transaction fees.

I'm not trying to hard-sell you - honestly, I'd love to just hear what you're looking for in a community platform. Understanding what creators actually need helps us build something better.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call this week? Even if KLASS isn't the right fit, I can share what I've learned from talking to dozens of creators in your space.

Either way, keep up the great work.

[Your name]
```

---

**Subject:** How [Similar Creator] built a $15K/month community

```

Random email, I know - but I think you'd find this interesting.

[Creator in similar niche] was in the same spot you're in now: great audience, lots of engagement, but revenue was unpredictable. They launched their own branded community platform and now do $15K/month in recurring revenue.

The kicker? They own everything - the platform, the member data, the brand. No Skool fees, no Teachable cuts.

I helped them set it up on KLASS (the platform I'm building for creators), and I'm wondering if something similar could work for you.

Not pitching a call or anything - but if you're curious how they structured it, I'm happy to share the breakdown.

Just reply "interested" and I'll send it over.

[Your name]
```

## Response Handling

### If They Say "Not Right Now"

```
Totally get it - timing is everything. I'll check back in a few months if that's cool?

In the meantime, if you ever have questions about community platforms in general, I'm always happy to help. Good luck with [their current project]!
```

### If They Say "Tell Me More"

```
Awesome! Here's the quick version:

KLASS is a white-label community platform - courses, community feed, events, resources - all on your own domain, fully branded as you.

No transaction fees (just Stripe's standard cut), and you actually own the platform vs renting space on Skool/Kajabi.

Would love to hop on a quick call to show you around and see if it fits what you're building. When works for you this week?
```

### If They Say "What's the Price?"

```
Good question! We're still finalizing pricing for our launch, but it's going to be a flat monthly fee - no percentage of your revenue, ever.

For creators in your range, we're looking at [price range] depending on features you need.

Happy to give you the full breakdown on a call - and honestly, for early adopters like you, there might be some flexibility. When's a good time to chat?
```

## What NOT to Do

❌ **Don't be spammy** - One thread per person, max 3 touches  
❌ **Don't copy-paste obviously** - Personalize every message  
❌ **Don't lead with price** - Lead with value and curiosity  
❌ **Don't trash competitors** - "We do X better" > "Y platform sucks"  
❌ **Don't be desperate** - If they're not interested, move on gracefully  
❌ **Don't follow up more than twice** - Respect is more important than a sale
